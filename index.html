About Our Engine

DynaMesh Studio is developing a proprietary procedural generation pipeline leveraging Python and Luau. We automate the creation of massive 3D province meshes for virtual environments, handling complex geometric configurations across global regions.

Dynamic Node Scaling

Our architecture includes a dynamic city scaling system that calculates spatial representation based on population parameters in real-time. Nodes scaling under 1,000,000 population render as highly optimized cylinders, while mega-nodes automatically transform into complex square geometries, optimizing both render overhead and visual fidelity.

Currently in active development. We are leveraging advanced LLM tooling to accelerate our geometry generation algorithms and Luau integrations.
